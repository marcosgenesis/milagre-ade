# tldr eval

Run this after the rewrite. Each check is pass or fail. Fix the draft and rerun until every check passes. For a detect request ("is this slop?"), skip the rewrite checks and confirm the report names each pattern with a quoted line and a short fix, without rewriting, scoring or claiming AI authorship.

## Information

1.  Does the rewrite keep every fact, number, command, `file:line` reference, error text, repro step, decision and reason from the original?
2.  Does it add no claims, examples, stats, quotes or opinions the original did not have?
3.  Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual padding, with no compression that removed a needed step?

## Shape

1.  Is the first line an action, an outcome or a verdict, not context?
2.  Is multi-step work a numbered list of bounded steps, each one action, with who and how long where that helps?
3.  Is every list five items or fewer, or split into "do now" and "later"?
4.  Is any completed work stated concretely enough that the reader can verify it in one try?
5.  Is there at most one tangent, marked "Separately:"?
6.  Is every error stated as cause plus fix, with no apology?
7.  If anything is left open, does the text end with one next action doable in under two minutes? Otherwise does it stop when the answer is done?
8.  Reading only the first and last line, does the reader know what to do next and what just happened?

## Voice

1.  Zero em dashes, en dashes or ` -- ` used as dashes in the final text?
2.  No emoji, exclamation marks or curly quotes?
3.  No word from the AI vocabulary list, unless quoted as an example?
4.  No chatbot residue, signposting, throat-clearing, faux-insight setup or weasel attribution?
5.  Are binary contrasts, negative parallelism, rule-of-three padding, colon reveals, "-ing" tails, puffery, metadiscourse, copula avoidance, synonym cycling and dramatic fragments gone?
6.  Are fake-profound kickers and recap endings deleted rather than rewritten into better ones?
7.  Is bold used only for labels the eye must find, with no headers over tiny sections and no bullets that should be prose?
8.  Do the remaining hedges each mark a real uncertainty?
9.  Does every generic sentence pass the portability test, or was it cut or made specific?
10. Would the original writer recognize the rewrite as their own voice, in first person where we speak as ourselves?
11. Does the sentence length vary, with no repeated sentence shapes or stacked punchy fragments?
